## 1. Percolation testing — narrative requires it, CG&B specifically excludes it

Scope of Work #022: *"Perform all required percolation tests prior to installation."*

CG&B's 05.12.26 proposal, under **SPECIFIC EXCLUSIONS** — not the boilerplate list —
item 4: *"Percolation testing is not included."*

That distinction matters. Boilerplate exclusions are ignored per the authority
hierarchy; a deliberate, specific exclusion against an express narrative requirement
is a real disagreement about what was bought. The exhibit carries the obligation.
**Confirm scope and price before buyout.**

## 2. Mobilizations — he priced one, the documents require several

CG&B: *"One mobilization/demobilization is included in pricing. Anything past that
will be charged at $6,500 per mobilization."*

The narrative requires *"multiple mobilizations… as may be required per the
Preliminary Construction Schedule and Logistics Plan,"* and the Attachment A's own
paragraph 94 is drafted **"multiple mobilizations"** by standing house format.

The turf field sequences **behind paving, track curb and concrete**. More than one
mobilization is not a hypothetical. This is unpriced exposure at $6,500 a trip.

## 3. ⚠️ Geotextile fabric may have fallen between this package and RFP-008

The narrative lists **geotextile underlayment** as a required component of the turf
system, and **L1.03 detail D** calls for geotextile over the subgrade and through
trenches.

**CG&B's itemized pricing does not list it.** His breakdown covers 4-inch base rock,
1,134 linear feet of nailer board, the Soteria pad and the Matrix Helix turf —
no geotextile line.

Meanwhile **TAB's (RFP-008) 05.22.26 re-priced homework carries a −$150,000 deduct
for "Delete Type II and geofabric from turf field"** — which reads as earthwork
dropping the fabric *on the assumption the turf package carries it.*

If neither carries it, it is a gap with a $150,000 credit already taken against it.
**Confirm the $1,139,000 includes geotextile under the full turf footprint.**

## 4. The "not the lowest bidder" flag is probably comparing a track price to a turf price

The package record flags CG&B's award against **AstroTurf at $609,151**. The
AstroTurf document filed under this package is titled **"Track Surfacing"** and
prices ~7,335 SY of **Rekortan BS** — the product named in Section 32 18 23.33's
manufacturer list for **RFP-021**, not this package — plus an add alternate for
turnkey track construction at $1,851,285.

The same document separately describes a 73,800 SF AstroTurf Rhino 46oz field, but
**no standalone turf-only total is captured anywhere.**

So the $609,151 being held against CG&B is very likely a **track** number. Verify
against whatever AstroTurf submitted on the RFP-021 bidder list before treating CG&B
as a high bidder. This flag should probably be withdrawn.

## 5. Nailer board — one bidder puts it in Concrete

Sprinturf's scope review states *"Concrete package should have the nailer board."*
The narrative and CG&B's own base bid both put the **1,134 linear feet** of perimeter
nailer board here, and the exhibit follows that.

Worth a scope-qa check that **RFP-030 does not also carry a nailer-board line** — the
same double-claim pattern already seen between RFP-031 and RFP-033 on masonry embeds,
and now three times over on foundations in this same batch.

## 6. Sprinturf's bid still does not split

Longstanding item; unchanged by this draft. **Tand** bid 016 and 022 bundled the same
way.
