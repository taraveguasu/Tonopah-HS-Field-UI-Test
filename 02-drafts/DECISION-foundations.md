# Decision memo — the same foundations are bought in three subcontracts

CORE Project No. 26-01-019 · Tonopah THS Sports Complex · 2026-08-12
Touches **RFP-030** (Sahara Concrete), **RFP-103** (Quantum Electric), **ITB-089** (YESCO)

> **The decision.** Who places the concrete for the four Musco light pole foundations
> and the scoreboard footing — the Concrete Subcontractor, or the two equipment subs
> whose prices currently include it?
>
> Until that is answered, **RFP-030, RFP-103 and ITB-089 should not issue.** They rank
> 8th, 2nd and 5th on the release log, so all three land inside the next five weeks.

At risk: **$62,452** in itemized duplication, plus an unpriced share of a $71,516
excavation alternate.

---

## A. Musco light pole foundations — SCOPE CONFLICT

Both exhibits claim the work. This is not only money; two subcontracts would be
signed for the same concrete.

> Excavate drilled piers and place concrete backfill around the Musco-furnished
> precast bases at sports field light poles, per E0-05.
> — **RFP-030**, Sahara Concrete, drafted exhibit

> Includes excavation, reinforcing, concrete, anchor bolts and drypack for the 4
> Musco sports field light pole foundations, per Musco's precast base.
> — **RFP-103**, Quantum Electric, drafted exhibit

Both are defensible reads, which is why both were drafted that way. Scope of Work
#030 assigns equipment footings to Concrete; Quantum priced the foundations as an
accepted alternate and carries the Musco interface. The documents do not resolve it.

| Where the money sits | Line | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| RFP-103 | ADD Alternate 06 — concrete foundations, 4 Musco poles | **$33,102** |
| RFP-103 | ADD Alternate 05 — 100% hard dig, all trenching *and foundations* | $71,516 |
| RFP-030 | inside the unitemized $1,186,334 site concrete lump | not broken out |

**Alternate 05 is only partly duplicated.** It covers hard-dig excavation for all of
Quantum's trenching, most of which is genuinely his. Only the share attributable to
the four pole piers overlaps RFP-030, and that share has to be priced separately —
it is not a clean $71,516 deduct.

## B. Scoreboard footing — MONEY ONLY

The scope is already right; only the dollars are in the wrong subcontract.

ITB-089's exhibit **explicitly excludes** "Scoreboard foundation excavation and
concrete," and RFP-030's exhibit claims it. That split is correct and consistent
with the 07.31.26 equipment-footings ruling.

But YESCO's carried $99,483 still contains the **$29,350 footing alternate** —
excavation, backfill, subbase, concrete, reinforcing, sleeves, anchors, pump truck
and auger. The exhibit gives the work away and the price keeps paying for it.

| ITB-089 · YESCO | Carried | If the alternate comes out |
|---|---:|---:|
| Base bid — scoreboards | $67,030 | $67,030 |
| Mobilization | $3,103 | $3,103 |
| Add alternate — footing | **$29,350** | — |
| **Subcontract amount** | **$99,483** | **$70,133** |

## The two ways to close it

Same choice applies to A and B. Pick once, apply to all three exhibits.

**Option 1 — recommended. Concrete keeps the work.**
RFP-030's exhibit stands as drafted. Deduct **$33,102** from RFP-103 and **$29,350**
from ITB-089, plus the pier share of Alternate 05.
Revised: RFP-103 **$1,041,439**, ITB-089 **$70,133**.

**Option 2 — the equipment subs keep it.**
RFP-103 and ITB-089 stand as priced. Strike the pole piers and scoreboard footing
from RFP-030 and take a matching deduct from Sahara. Reverses the 07.31.26 ruling
and puts three crews on the same pour sequence.

**Why option 1.** Sahara has the crew, the rebar, the pump truck and the pour
sequence — the footings are already on his critical path. Neither YESCO nor Quantum
self-performs concrete, so under option 2 both sub it back at a markup and CORE
coordinates three mobilizations to the same pour. Option 1 also keeps the 07.31.26
ruling intact rather than carving two exceptions into it.

## One thing to verify before deducting

**Sahara's footings are not itemized.** The pole piers and scoreboard footing sit
inside a single unbroken **$1,186,334** site-concrete line, and his carried amount is
already **$239,996.16 below his own proposal** with nothing on file documenting the
reduction.

So option 1 rests on an assumption worth confirming in writing: that this concrete is
still inside Sahara's number. If the undocumented reduction removed it, deducting
from the other two would leave the work bought by nobody.

## What to do

1. **Confirm the pole piers and scoreboard footing are inside Sahara's carried
   $1,278,459.84**, ideally as a written breakout of the site-concrete line. This also
   starts closing the $239,996.16 reconciliation, which RFP-030 cannot issue without.
2. **Get Quantum to price the pier share of Alternate 05 separately** from his
   trenching hard dig, so the excavation deduct is a real number, not an allocation.
3. **Rule on A and B together** — all three exhibits and cover sheets are then revised
   in one pass.
4. **Handle Musco's warning on its own.** Their 05.11.26 email says the drawn
   pole-foundation detail predates both the added track lighting and the geotech, and
   that structural now needs a reinforced precast base with rebar "none of the
   contractors are including in their bid." That is a price exposure on whoever owns
   the foundation. It does not change who should own it and should not hold up this
   decision.
