## 2. The spec says remove the slabs. Everything else says leave them.

**Section 02 41 00 ¶3.01.I:** *"Remove concrete slabs on grade within site
boundaries."*

Against that: the Scope of Work narrative (*"Slabs-on-grade to remain"*) and **every
ES demolition sheet** — AD1.11 through AD1.16, note 1: *"EXISTING BUILDING TO BE
DEMOLISHED, EXISTING SLABS TO REMAIN IN PLACE."*

The exhibit follows the narrative and drawings, which are the more specific and more
recently confirmed instruments. But this is a **generic spec section contradicting
six sheets on a large quantity of concrete**, and it should get a documented ruling
rather than resting on a drafter's reading.

## 4. The asbestos survey is the contractual baseline — and it is from 2023

**September 19, 2023**. The staged file matches. Both the narrative and every bidder

On a building that has sat abandoned for three years, that is a live exposure, not a
formality. Nothing to fix in the exhibit — worth knowing where the risk sits.
