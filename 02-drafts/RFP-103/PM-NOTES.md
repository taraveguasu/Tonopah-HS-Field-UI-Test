## 1. ⚠️ Alternate 06 buys concrete that RFP-030 also buys

**$33,102 of this carried amount is concrete foundations for the 4 Musco light
poles.** RFP-030's exhibit independently carries *"Excavate drilled piers and place
concrete backfill around the Musco-furnished precast bases at sports field light
poles, per E0-05."*

Alternate 05's **$71,516** "100% hard dig" electrical excavation overlaps the same
pier excavation.

Together with the identical problem on ITB-089 (scoreboard footings, $29,350), that
is **$62,452 of duplicated concrete** across three packages, before the excavation
overlap. **Decide the boundary before any of the three issue.** Full write-up in
`02-drafts/RFP-030/PM-NOTES.md` §1.

## 2. ⚠️ Two deducts did NOT come out — and one filename says otherwise

Two of Quantum's priced deducts do **not** appear in the reconciliation above, which
means **both scopes remain fully included** at full value:

## 3. Network switches — the GMP and the Project Manual disagree

The Basis of GMP excludes *"Active Network Equipment (Servers, Switches, etc.)"*
project-wide. **Section 27 00 00 ¶1.1.A.1 expressly requires the Contractor to
furnish and install network switches and a UPS.**

Drafted per the GMP, which supersedes as an addendum under your 08.04.26 ruling. But
these are two live contract documents pointing opposite ways on real money, and the
spec is the one the sub will be held to at closeout. Confirm.

## 4. Musco's own email says the drawn pole foundation is out of date

Musco wrote on **05.11.26 — the day before bid opening** — that the drawn
pole-foundation detail predates both the added track-lighting fixtures and the
geotech, and that structural now requires a **reinforced precast base with rebar
"none of the contractors are including in their bid."**

That is a vendor telling us, in writing, that every bid on the table is light. It
lands directly against the $33,102 Alternate 06 pickup in §1. Musco is single-source
— there is no recovery by rebidding — and this package already defines the
procurement critical path. **Resolve this one first.**
