## 1. ⚠️ Foundations are potentially bought twice, in two other packages

This is a cross-package finding and it is the reason to read RFP-103 and ITB-089
alongside this one. **This exhibit carries both of the following:**

- *"Provide cast-in-place footings for the Scoreboard, Press Box, Prefabricated
  Ticket Booth, Bleacher footings/grade beams, and Track & Field Athletic
  Equipment…"*
- *"Excavate drilled piers and place concrete backfill around the Musco-furnished
  precast bases at sports field light poles, per E0-05."*

Both are correct per Scope of Work #030 and the 07.31.26 ruling on athletic
equipment footings. **But the other two packages' carried amounts include the same
concrete:**

| Package | Line inside its carried amount | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| RFP-103 Quantum Electric | ADD Alternate 06 — concrete foundations for the 4 Musco poles | 33,102 |
| ITB-089 YESCO | Add alternate — scoreboard footing excavation and concrete | 29,350 |
| | **Exposure** | **62,452** |

Plus part of RFP-103's Alternate 05 ($71,516, "100% hard dig" electrical excavation),
which overlaps the pier excavation above.

**Do not issue RFP-030, RFP-103 and ITB-089 without deciding this.** Either the work
is Sahara's and the other two amounts come down, or it moves and this exhibit's items
come out with a deduct. Right now all three subcontracts would pay for it.

the base-bid civil drawings. The Basis of Proposal replaced it with an **RCP flared

confirm **which package now carries the RCP flared end** — it currently has no home
in this exhibit.

## 3. Flagpole count and the ITB-078 boundary

separately priced Alternate 030.01 at $16,500. The Basis of Proposal reduces the

footing*, or whether this package still sets the footing. The exhibit carries **one**
flagpole footing and installation, matching the reduced count. Confirm the boundary
with ITB-078 — and correct Sahara's base price for the two deleted bases either way.
